Admissions

Welcome to Step Ahead Nursery and Primary School. We welcome parents and guardians who wish to give their children a good foundation for their education.

How to Apply

Parents or guardians can visit the school and collect an admission form fill in the required information and submit the form to the school office.

Requirements

  • Completed admission form
  • Previous school report, where applicable
  • Birth certificate or proof of birth
  • Passport-size photographs

Visit Our School

Parents and guardians are welcome to visit our school for more information about admission, fees and school programmes.
